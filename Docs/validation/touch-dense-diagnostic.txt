The continuity source includes the base harness with main renamed touch_main.
For all four models, four strikers and velocities 16/64/127, it renders each of
positions .05, .2242, .23, .5, .77, .7758, .95 at position +/- .00001. It sums
the squared close-output sample differences over the complete 180 ms and divides
by the close-output energy of a matching center strike. The maximum ratio,
expressed as 10*log10, measures local waveform continuity without amplifying
relative errors inside an almost silent spatial null. Its include path is
/Users/vojta/Dev/reason-rack-extension/SDK/Examples/Maremba/Tests/TouchResponseTest.cpp;
adjust that path for a different checkout location.
