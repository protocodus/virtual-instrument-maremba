01 · Material

The same repertoire across four models makes their tonal differences easier to compare. All audio is synthesized by Maremba.

02 · Contact

Compare the centre, edge and fundamental node of a bar, or move along the playable end toward the tip of a tine. Same-note Kalimba studies isolate four pluck styles. Touch studies set Variance and Artifacts to zero; subtle intrinsic variation remains.

03 · Dynamics

Five C4 strikes rise from velocity 16 to 127 at fixed gain. Repeated-hit studies keep every note at velocity 90, so you can hear natural differences between identical inputs. No mastering compression or per-hit normalization.

04 · Idiom

Original phrases play the instruments' own techniques: rolls on the mod wheel, a hand over the Kalimba's sound holes, the Bala 7-equal and 5-equal pentatonic tunings, and keys stopped by the hand on release (Note-off Damp).
